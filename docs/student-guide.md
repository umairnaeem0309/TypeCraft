# TypeCraft Student Guide

How to use TypeCraft to learn touch typing.

---

## 1. Choosing your profile

1. Start TypeCraft.
2. On the Main Menu, click **Choose Profile**.
3. Tap your name card. If you do not have one, ask your teacher to create it.
4. You return to the Main Menu with your name at the top.

---

## 2. Picking a lesson

1. From the Main Menu, click **Lessons**.
2. Scroll through the lesson cards. Locked lessons are dimmed and cannot be opened.
3. Click an unlocked lesson.

---

## 3. Choosing a mode

Before the lesson starts, pick a mode:

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Lock on Error** | A wrong key is ignored and counted as a mistake. Keep typing the right key to continue. |
| **Backspace** | Mistakes stay on screen; press Backspace to fix them. |
| **Free Advance** | Characters advance as soon as you press any key, even wrong ones. |

Ask your teacher which mode to use. Newer students usually start with **Lock on Error**.

---

## 4. Reading the lesson screen

The screen shows:

- **Target text** — the line(s) you are typing.
- **Cursor** — a caret and a soft block mark the next character to type.
- **Keyboard** — the on-screen keyboard highlights the next key and the finger to use.
- **HUD** (top of the screen) — timer, accuracy, WPM, combo, and stars earned so far.
- **Hint** — a caption above the keyboard names the finger for the next key.

A space is shown as a small middle dot so you always know it is there.

---

## 5. Keyboard guidance

- The key you need next is highlighted.
- For capital letters, both the letter and the opposite-hand **Shift** key light up.
- The finger name appears above the keyboard, for example: *“Press T with your left index finger.”*
- Use the correct finger for each key. The lesson is designed to build muscle memory.

---

## 6. Stars, XP, and badges

When you finish a lesson you earn:

- **1–3 stars** based on accuracy.
- **XP** based on accuracy and speed.
- **Badges** when you meet special goals (first lesson, 100 % accuracy, 30 WPM, etc.).
- **Streak bonus** on the first lesson you finish each day (5–25 XP, up to 5 days in a row).

Your level grows from 1 to 10 as you collect XP. The only way to reach Level 10 is to practise regularly — replaying lessons, earning badges, and keeping a daily streak.

---

## 7. Finishing and leaving a lesson

- **Completing the lesson** shows the Results screen with stars, XP, and a message.
- **Pressing Escape** saves your progress as an incomplete attempt. You can continue later.
- **Closing the window** also saves your incomplete attempt, as long as you have typed at least one key.

If the computer loses power, a checkpoint is saved automatically every few seconds, so most of your work is preserved.
